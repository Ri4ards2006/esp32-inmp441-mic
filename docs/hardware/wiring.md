# Hardware Wiring & Interface Specification

This document provides complete electrical specifications, pin mappings, and physical design recommendations for interfacing the **ESP32-WROOM-32D** development board with the **InvenSense INMP441** omnidirectional I2S digital MEMS microphone.

---

## 1. Pin Connection Matrix

| INMP441 Pin | Signal Name | ESP32 Pin | GPIO | Pin Function / Direction | Electrical Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VDD** | Power (3.3V) | 3V3 | - | Power supply (Input) | Operating range: 1.8V - 3.3V (**Do NOT connect to 5V**) |
| **GND** | Ground | GND | - | System Ground | Common ground reference |
| **L/R** | Channel Select | GND | - | Hardware channel config | **GND = Left Channel (Mono)**; VDD = Right Channel |
| **SD** | Serial Data | D32 | GPIO 32 | I2S Data Out (Mic -> ESP32) | 24-bit 2's complement audio word |
| **WS** | Word Select | D15 | GPIO 15 | I2S Word Select / LRCLK (ESP32 -> Mic) | Clock rate = Sample Rate (e.g., 16 kHz) |
| **SCK** | Serial Clock | D14 | GPIO 14 | I2S Bit Clock / BCLK (ESP32 -> Mic) | Clock rate = $f_{sample} \times 32 \times 2$ |

## 3. Signal Roles & Protocol Timing

### 3.1 Serial Clock (SCK / BCLK)
- **Source**: Generated by the ESP32 (acting as **I2S Master**).
- **Frequency**:
  $$\text{SCK} = f_{\text{sample}} \times \text{Bits Per Channel} \times \text{Number of Channels}$$
  For 16 kHz audio with 32-bit slot width (stereo clock frame):
  $$\text{SCK} = 16\,000 \times 32 \times 2 = 1.024\text{ MHz}$$
- The microphone latches and shifts out audio bits on the falling edge of SCK.

### 3.2 Word Select (WS / LRCLK)
- **Source**: Generated by the ESP32 (I2S Master).
- **Frequency**: Equal to the sampling frequency ($f_{\text{sample}}$ = 16 kHz).
- **Polarity**:
  - `WS = Low`: Left channel data window.
  - `WS = High`: Right channel data window.
- Because `L/R` is tied to **GND**, the INMP441 drives the `SD` line only during the Left channel subframe (when `WS` is Low). During the Right channel subframe, the microphone tri-states (high-impedance) its data output.

### 3.3 Serial Data (SD)
- **Source**: Driven by the INMP441.
- **Word Format**: 24-bit sign-extended two's complement integer, transmitted MSB-first.
- The 24 active bits are placed within a 32-bit I2S subframe (occupying the 24 most significant bits, with trailing zeros).

## 4. Hardware Guidelines & Noise Reduction

1. **Supply Decoupling**:
   - Place a **0.1 µF (100 nF) ceramic bypass capacitor** between VDD and GND as close as physically possible to the INMP441 board pins.
   - If powering the ESP32 from USB, high-frequency switching noise on the 3.3V rail can couple into the microphone analog front-end. If noise is observed, consider adding a 10 µF electrolytic or tantalum capacitor in parallel.

2. **Wiring Distance**:
   - Keep jumper wires between the ESP32 and INMP441 **under 15 cm (6 inches)**. High-speed clock lines (SCK at >1 MHz) can suffer from capacitive loading, reflections, and clock jitter over long ribbon cables.

3. **Grounding**:
   - Ensure a solid, low-impedance ground connection. Avoid daisy-chaining ground through high-current devices (such as RGB LED strips or relays).

4. **ESP32 Strapping Pins**:
   - `GPIO 15` (used for `WS`) is an ESP32 strapping pin (MTDO) that controls ROM boot messaging output during reset. It has an internal pull-up/pull-down during boot. Driving `WS` from `GPIO 15` in software after boot causes no conflict, but verify your dev kit boots smoothly. If your board fails to flash with GPIO 15 connected, disconnect it during upload or switch to GPIO 25.
